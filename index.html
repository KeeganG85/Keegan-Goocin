THE MÍA STORY

Mía means mine.

Gleam started with one rule: work on every car the way you’d work on your own. Not the fastest way. Not the cheapest way. The way you’d want it done if the keys had your name on them.

Miami doesn’t forgive a car — sun, salt air, and summer storms wear down paint faster than almost anywhere else. That’s the fight Gleam was built for, and it’s the standard on every job, mobile van or studio bay: every car, treated like it’s mía.
