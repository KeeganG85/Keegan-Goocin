# Gleam Auto Detailing — Visual Identity

*Phase 2 of 5 — Logo, colour palette, and typography. Built on the Phase 1 Brand Strategy.*

---

## Logo Concepts

### Concept 1 — "The Reflection Mark"

- **Icon:** A single chrome-gradient arc, drawn as if it were the specular highlight sweeping across a freshly corrected panel — not a full circle, just the curve of light a detailer chases with a polisher. It reads equally as a droplet of water beading on ceramic-coated paint.
- **Wordmark:** "GLEAM" set in a tight, geometric sans serif, wide tracking, all caps — confident and architectural, like brushed metal signage on a marina building.
- **Mía integration:** A fine-line signature script of "mía" sits beneath the wordmark at roughly a third of its size, as a secondary mark — used on invoices, the founder's signature line, and the interior of vehicle door decals, never competing with GLEAM for primary visual weight.

### Concept 2 — "The Correction Pass"

- **Icon:** Three thin parallel arcs of decreasing opacity, representing the layered passes of a paint-correction polisher — a literal record of the compounding, polishing, and finishing stages compressed into one clean mark. Reads as motion and depth simultaneously.
- **Wordmark:** "Gleam" in a refined sans serif with subtly rounded terminals — warmer and more approachable than Concept 1, sentence case rather than all caps.
- **Mía integration:** "mía" is worked in as an integrated tagline directly under the wordmark, in a light italic: *Gleam. Mía.* — functioning as a spoken pairing, the way a founder would actually say the brand aloud.

### Concept 3 — "The Sunline"

- **Icon:** A minimal horizon mark — a single horizontal line bisected by a small radiant burst, evoking the exact moment sun hits a hood at golden hour on the causeway. Doubles as an abstract "G".
- **Wordmark:** "GLEAM" in a high-contrast serif-sans hybrid — thin strokes with sharp, slightly flared terminals, giving it a jewelry-label quality rather than an automotive-shop one.
- **Mía integration:** "mía" appears as a hand-signature flourish, used exclusively as a stamp on completed work (a small foil sticker in the door jamb, a closing mark on final photos) — a literal signature confirming the work is done and stands behind it.

**Recommendation:** Concept 2, "The Correction Pass." It's the only mark that visually encodes the actual craft (the correction stages) rather than borrowing generic automotive or sun iconography, and pairing "Gleam. Mía." as spoken language gives every application — from a decal to an Instagram bio — an instant, ownable voice.

## Colour Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Inspiration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary 1 | Sun Chrome | `#E8E2D6` | Polished metal catching direct Miami sunlight — warm, not white |
| Primary 2 | Deep Bay | `#0B1F2A` | Biscayne Bay water at depth, near-black with a blue undertone |
| Secondary 1 | Wet Asphalt | `#2E3538` | Studio bay floor after a rinse, a working neutral for text and structure |
| Secondary 2 | Brushed Steel | `#9BA3A6` | Polished metal in shadow, used for dividers, captions, secondary UI |
| Accent | Causeway Gold | `#C9973B` | Late-afternoon Miami light glancing off chrome trim — used sparingly, never as a base colour |

Deliberately absent: teal and flamingo pink. If either is ever used, it is restricted to a single micro-accent (a single icon stroke, never a background or a headline colour) so the palette reads as polished chrome under sun, not souvenir-shop Miami.

## Typography

### Pairing 1 — Structural / Body

- **Headings & UI:** *Söhne* or *Neue Haas Grotesk* (elegant grotesk sans, tight tracking on headlines, generous tracking on labels and eyebrow text).
- **Body copy:** *Inter* or *Suisse Int'l* at a slightly loosened tracking for long-form readability on mobile.

### Pairing 2 — The Mía Signature Accent

- **Signature/script:** *Reforma* or *Canela Deck Italic* for the word "mía" wherever it appears as a standalone accent — taglines, the logo lockup, section dividers on the website.
- **Alternative refined serif (if script feels too soft for a given context, e.g. embroidery):** *Canela* regular, used only for the single word "mía," never for full sentences.

---

## Usage Direction

- **Dark backgrounds (Deep Bay `#0B1F2A`):** Wordmark in Sun Chrome, "mía" signature in Causeway Gold. This is the primary hero and social-post treatment — it reads like chrome and gold under a night sky over the water.
- **Light backgrounds (Sun Chrome `#E8E2D6` or white):** Wordmark in Deep Bay, "mía" signature in Wet Asphalt or Causeway Gold depending on contrast needs. Used for print, invoices, and studio signage.
- **Automotive contexts:**
  - *Vehicle wraps/van livery:* Wordmark in brushed-metal foil finish (physically reflective, not printed gold) on Deep Bay panels — the van itself becomes a demonstration of the correction-and-coating work.
  - *Window decals:* Single-colour Sun Chrome or white vinyl, icon only, sized small and placed low on the rear glass — a quiet signature, not a billboard.
  - *Embroidered apparel:* Icon plus wordmark in a single thread colour (Sun Chrome on Deep Bay uniforms), with "mía" embroidered in a contrasting gold thread only on founder/lead-technician apparel, reinforcing it as a personal signature rather than a staff-wide slogan.
