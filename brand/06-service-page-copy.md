## Package 1 — Mía's Radiance

**Positioning:** The refresh that makes a daily driver feel like it did the day you brought it home.

**What's included:**
- Full hand wash and decontamination (chemical + clay bar)
- Exterior wax or sealant for a durable, glossy finish
- Interior vacuum, wipe-down, and leather/vinyl conditioning
- Window and glass detail, inside and out
- Tire and trim dressing

**The process:**
We start with a two-bucket hand wash, no automated brushes near your paint. A clay bar lifts embedded contamination — the rail dust and brake fines you can feel but not see — before a sealant goes on to lock in gloss and buy you weeks of protection against Miami's humidity and salt air. Inside, leather gets conditioned, not just wiped, so it doesn't dry and crack under the dash heat.

**The emotional payoff:** You get back in and it smells right, feels right, and catches light the way it did on delivery day — without giving up your whole Saturday.

**Ideal client:** The daily-driver owner who wants their car looking sharp for the week ahead without committing to a full correction.

## Package 2 — Full Gleam Ceramic

**Positioning:** Real paint correction, sealed under a ceramic system that actually holds up to a Miami summer.

**What's included:**
- One- or two-stage machine paint correction (removes swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation)
- Professional-grade ceramic coating application (multi-year protection)
- Wheel and caliper detail
- Interior deep clean and protection
- Paint inspection under LED raking light, before and after

**The process:**
Correction happens in passes, not one pass and done. We compound out the deeper defects first, then refine with a finishing polish until the paint reads clean under raking light — no hazing, no hologramming. Once the surface is level, the ceramic goes on in a controlled environment and cures for the manufacturer-specified window before the car leaves our hands, so the ceramic bonds properly instead of curing half-finished in your driveway.

**The emotional payoff:** Water doesn't sit on your hood anymore — it beads and runs. You stop flinching at every valet parking job because you know the paint underneath is actually protected, not just glossy on top.

**Ideal client:** The owner who's tired of re-waxing every few months and wants years of protection against sun, salt, and daily wear.

## Package 3 — The Miami Rebirth

**Positioning:** Full correction, full protection, full restoration — for the car that deserves to look the way it did the day it left the factory.

**What's included:**
- Multi-stage paint correction (up to 3 passes: heavy compound, refinement, finishing polish)
- Premium ceramic coating with extended cure protocol
- Full interior deep restoration (leather reconditioning, headliner care, trim restoration)
- Engine bay detail
- Headlight restoration
- Complete before/after documentation

**The process:**
This is a multi-day build, not a single appointment. Paint gets measured for clear coat thickness before correction begins, so we know exactly how much we can safely remove. Each pass is inspected under raking light before moving to the next. Interior restoration runs in parallel — leather is deep-conditioned in stages, not soaked once and called done. By the time ceramic goes on, the surface underneath is as close to factory-flat as the car has been since it left the line.

**The emotional payoff:** You open the garage door and, for a moment, it's the car you first fell in love with — not the car that's absorbed a decade of Miami sun.

**Ideal client:** The collector, exotic owner, or long-time owner ready to fully restore a vehicle's finish before a sale, a show, or simply for their own pride of ownership.

## FAQ

**Q: How far in advance do I need to book?**
A: Mobile appointments typically book within 3–5 days. Full correction packages (Full Gleam Ceramic, The Miami Rebirth) often need a week or more of lead time, since ceramic curing requires a dedicated block on our schedule.

**Q: What's the difference between mobile and studio service?**
A: Mobile brings the full Mía's Radiance and most Full Gleam Ceramic jobs to your driveway or garage. The Miami Rebirth and any multi-day correction requiring controlled temperature and humidity for proper ceramic curing is studio-only.

**Q: Is ceramic coating safe on exotic paint, matte finishes, or vehicle wraps?**
A: Yes — we adjust product and process by surface. Matte finishes and wraps get matte-specific, non-glossing ceramic formulas, and we always test on a low-visibility panel first on any exotic or specialty finish.

**Q: How long does a full correction and ceramic job take?**
A: Full Gleam Ceramic typically runs 1–2 days. The Miami Rebirth runs 2–4 days depending on paint condition and interior restoration scope.

**Q: Will paint correction remove all of my car's imperfections?**
A: Most swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation, yes. Deep scratches that have gone through the clear coat need touch-up paint, not correction — we'll tell you honestly during inspection which category your car falls into before any work begins.
