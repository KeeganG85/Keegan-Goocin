# Gleam Auto Detailing — Brand Voice & Messaging

*Phase 2 of 5 — Voice and messaging system. Built on the Phase 1 Brand Strategy, developed alongside Phase 2 Visual Identity.*

---

## Brand Voice Chart

| Tone | Vocabulary | Rhythm |
|---|---|---|
| **Confident** — states process and outcome plainly, never hedges | **Use:** correction, cure, panel, precision, mía, gleam, honest wear, protected | **Short declarative sentences for claims** ("We correct the paint. We don't hide it.") |
| **Warm** — speaks like a person who's proud of the work, not a script | **Avoid:** showroom shine, like-new, mint condition, best in Miami, unbeatable prices | **Slightly longer sentences for sensory or emotional beats**, then a short line to land the point |
| **Precise** — technical enough to be credible to an exotic-car owner | **Use (sparingly, natural):** claro, mía, con cuidado, listo | **No exclamation points** — confidence is carried by word choice and pacing, not punctuation |

## Elevator Pitch

*(30 words)*

Gleam is mobile and studio detailing for Miami's most cared-for cars — paint correction, ceramic coating, and interior restoration, delivered the way you'd treat your own. Because to us, it's *mía*.

---

## Tagline Options

1. **"Yours. Corrected. Ours to protect."** — *Emotional angle: ownership handed back and forth, the promise of a partnership, not a transaction.*
2. **"Gleam like it's mía."** — *Emotional angle: the most literal pairing of the brand name and signature — playful, direct, quotable.*
3. **"Every car, mía."** — *Emotional angle: the shortest possible statement of the entire brand ethos — fits a decal or a bio in four words.*
4. **"Miami sun. Mía care."** — *Emotional angle: locates the brand geographically and emotionally in one breath, leans into the bilingual rhythm.*
5. **"Not detailed. Gleamed."** — *Emotional angle: reframes the category itself — positions Gleam as a different verb, not just a better version of a commodity service.*

**Recommended primary tagline: "Every car, mía."**
It's the only option short enough to survive every application in the brand system — a window decal, an Instagram bio, a business card — while still carrying the entire emotional thesis (this car is treated like it's mine) without needing the word "Gleam" to complete the thought.

## Core Messaging Pillars

### 1. Trust
**Pillar statement:** Gleam earns access to a client's car the same way it earns access to their home — by being worth the key.

- Proof point: Every technician is trained and supervised directly by the founder; no rotating subcontracted crews.
- Proof point: Product and process are disclosed by name on every invoice — no vague "premium package" line items.

### 2. Passion
**Pillar statement:** This is a founder-led craft, not a franchise script — every panel gets the same attention the founder gives the car in their own garage.

- Proof point: Every job is inspected under raking light before it's marked complete, not just wiped and photographed.
- Proof point: Founder personally reviews before/after documentation on every ceramic coating job.

### 3. Transformation
**Pillar statement:** A Gleam appointment doesn't just clean a car — it resets the relationship between an owner and the vehicle they fell for in the first place.

- Proof point: Multi-stage paint correction measurably removes swirl marks and light scratching, not just adds gloss on top of them.
- Proof point: Interior rejuvenation restores leather, headliners, and trim rather than masking wear with fragrance and vacuuming alone.
