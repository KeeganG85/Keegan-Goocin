# Gleam Auto Detailing — Brand Strategy & Positioning

*Phase 1 of 5 — Foundation document. All subsequent brand and website work derives from this page.*

---

## Mission

Gleam Auto Detailing exists to treat every car that crosses our path the way we treat our own — *mía* — restoring it to the standard its owner fell in love with the day they bought it.

## Vision

To be the name Miami's most discerning car owners say without hesitation when someone asks who touches their paint.

## Five Core Values

1. **Ownership.** We work on every vehicle as if the title were already in our name.
2. **Precision.** Success is measured in microns of clear coat removed, not minutes on a invoice.
3. **Discretion.** Access to a client's home, garage, and exotic is a trust we protect like a vault combination.
4. **Correction, not concealment.** We fix the paint. We don't fog a swirl mark with filler and call it done.
5. **Warmth.** Technical mastery delivered with real Miami hospitality — never corporate, never cold.

## Brand Personality

1. **Meticulous** — every panel gets inspected under raking light before it's called finished, not just wiped down and photographed.
2. **Confident** — we quote exact process and product, never vague promises, because we know what the work actually does.
3. **Warm** — clients get a handshake and a conversation, not a drop-off ticket and a wait number.
4. **Precise** — our language matches our work: correction stages, cure times, and film thickness, not "magic."
5. **Sun-bright** — the brand carries Miami's light in its energy: alert, vivid, never sleepy or muted.

## Unique Value Proposition

*(38 words)*

Gleam brings studio-grade paint correction and ceramic protection to your driveway or our Miami studio, treating every exotic, luxury, and daily driver like our own — *mía* — built to outlast relentless sun, salt air, and owners who notice everything.

## Target Audience Personas

| Persona | Age Range | Vehicle Type | Motivation | Objection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marco** | 35–50 | Exotic / supercar (Lamborghini, Ferrari, McLaren) | Protecting a six-figure investment; status among peers at the club or the marina | "Who's actually touching my car — do they understand what this paint is worth?" |
| **Camila** | 28–42 | Luxury SUV / sedan (Range Rover, Mercedes, Tesla) | Time scarcity; wants the car to match her professional image without spending a Saturday on it | "Is mobile detailing actually as thorough as a shop, or am I paying for convenience alone?" |
| **Diego** | 45–62 | Classic / collector car | Preservation of originality and pride of a decades-long relationship with the car | "Will a modern detailer respect vintage paint, patina, and original trim instead of over-correcting it?" |

## Competitive Differentiator

Most Miami detailers sell volume: a wash, a wax, a rotating crew, a fixed price list. Gleam sells outcome and ownership — multi-stage paint correction and true ceramic systems, personally overseen from first inspection to final cure, delivered with the same care the founder gives the one car parked in their own garage. Competitors detail cars. Gleam treats them like *mía*.

## Brand Story

*(150 words)*

Miami doesn't forgive a car. The sun bleaches clear coat by three o'clock, salt air off the causeway creeps into every seam, and a summer storm can etch water spots into hood paint before the humidity even breaks. Gleam was built for that fight — and for the particular ache of watching something you love get worn down by a city that never slows down for it.

*Mía* is the word for that ache, and the promise against it. It means mine. It's what you say about the car you'd never hand to just anyone — and it's the standard applied to every vehicle that comes through Gleam's doors, mobile van, or studio bay. Chrome brought back to a mirror under blazing light. Leather conditioned until it smells the way it did on day one. Every car treated, quite simply, like it's *mía*.
